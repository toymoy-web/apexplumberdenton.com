Water Heater Installation & Replacement in Denton, TX

When a tank is leaking from the body, or a 12-year-old heater needs its third repair, replacement is usually the better spend. Water heater installation in Denton, TX covers sizing, removal of the old unit, code updates and hookup, and a failed tank can be swapped on an emergency basis when the timing matters.

Hot water heater replacement: choosing the right unit

Water heater replacement means removing the old unit and installing a new one sized to the household. The first decision is the type. Each has a real trade-off. Heat pump models are often sold as hybrid water heaters, because they pair a heat pump with backup electric elements.

The plumber should confirm gas line capacity, venting and electrical supply before recommending a type.
TypeGood fit forWatch out for
Gas tank.Homes with an existing Atmos Energy gas line and venting.Venting must meet current code, which older garage installs sometimes do not.

If your current unit is under about eight years old and the tank itself is dry, a repair may still make more sense. Our water heater repair page covers that decision.

Sizing a water heater for a Denton household

Tank size is usually picked by the number of people and bathrooms. Two people often do fine with a 40-gallon tank. A family of four with two or more showers running in the morning usually needs 50 gallons or more.

Winter changes the math here. Water entering a Denton home in January is far colder than in summer, so a tank takes longer to heat each refill. A heater that barely keeps up in October can fall short on the coldest mornings.

Tankless units are sized by flow instead of storage. The question is how many fixtures run at once, measured in gallons per minute, and how much the unit must raise the incoming water temperature. Upkeep after install, mainly yearly descaling, has its own page.

What a replacement visit includes

A standard tank swap is usually a same-day job. Here is the order of work.

  • Shut off water, gas or power, then drain and disconnect the old tank.
  • Check the gas line, venting, electrical circuit and water connections for code issues.
  • Set the new unit on a stand or pan as required, and connect supply, gas or power, and venting.
  • Install a new temperature and pressure relief valve discharge line to an approved termination.
  • Fill, purge air, light or energize the unit, and test for leaks and correct temperature.
  • Haul away the old heater and explain the new unit's maintenance schedule.

Permits, code and the 2021 IPC

Inside Denton city limits, plumbing work is held to the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), adopted by the City in 2022 with regional amendments. A water heater replacement touches water, gas or electrical supply and venting, so it may require a permit and an inspection.

Common items an inspector checks include the relief valve discharge line, gas venting and clearances, a drain pan where the heater could damage the building if it leaks, and seismic or tipping restraint where required by the adopted code.

A replacement is also a chance to fix old shortcuts. A missing shutoff valve, an undersized gas connector or a vent sloped the wrong way is far easier to correct while the old unit is out.

Replacement patterns across Denton

Older closet installs near the Square often need venting and drain pan updates when the tank is replaced, so those jobs take longer than a garage swap.

In Robson Ranch and other newer neighborhoods, owners more often ask about switching to tankless or heat pump units when the first tank wears out.

What drives the cost of a new water heater

The unit is only part of the price. These factors decide the rest.

  • Type and size of the new unit. Tankless and heat pump units cost more upfront than standard tanks.
  • Switching fuel or type. Moving from tank to tankless, or electric to gas, adds line, vent or circuit work.
  • Location. Attic and closet installs take more labor than an open garage.
  • Code updates found during the swap, such as venting or a drain pan.
  • Permit and inspection fees where required.

Non-emergency replacement quotes are free. If the old tank has failed and water is on the floor, the emergency swap is priced before work begins.
