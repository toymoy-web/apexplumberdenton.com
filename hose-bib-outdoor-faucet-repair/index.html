Hose Bib & Outdoor Faucet Repair in Denton, TX

An outdoor faucet that drips all day. A handle that will not shut off. A spray inside the wall the first time you use it in spring. Hose bib repair in Denton, TX covers worn valves, freeze-split spigots and upgrades to frost-free models.

Signs an outdoor faucet needs attention

A hose bib is a small part, but a failed one can leak into a wall for weeks.

  • Drips from the spout with the handle fully closed.
  • Water from the handle stem when it is turned on.
  • Water inside the wall or garage the first time the faucet is used after a freeze.
  • Weak flow from scale or a partly failed valve.
  • A handle that spins without opening or closing.

That third sign is the one to take seriously. A freeze-split valve can leak inside the wall where you will not see it.

Why hose bibs fail in Denton

A hose bib, also called a spigot or sillcock, is the outdoor faucet on an exterior wall. Hose bib repair covers replacing the washer or cartridge, or the whole valve when it has split.

Freezes do most of the damage. Denton and the rest of North Texas get only a few hard freezes most winters, but a hose left connected traps water in the valve, and that water expands when it freezes. The split often happens inside the wall, and the leak only shows once the faucet is used again.

Summer adds the other stress. In Denton County, irrigation, pool top-offs and outdoor projects put outdoor faucets through heavy use from May through September, and the city's watering schedules mean they run hard on set days. Scale from mineral-heavy water wears washers and seats faster.

Standard versus frost-free hose bibs

A frost-free model is the usual upgrade when a spigot has split.

Even frost-free models can split if a hose is left attached, since water cannot drain out.

What to do before a hard freeze

These steps take a few minutes and prevent most outdoor faucet damage.

  • Disconnect every garden hose from outdoor faucets.
  • Fit an insulated foam cover over each spigot.
  • If the house has an interior shutoff for outdoor faucets, close it and open the outside faucet to drain.
  • Drain or insulate above-ground irrigation backflow assemblies as well.

If a faucet did split and water is pouring into a wall, read what to do about a split pipe. Irrigation assemblies are covered under backflow testing.

Outdoor faucet trouble around Denton

Freeze-split spigots show up most on homes with large yards and long hose runs, from the south Denton subdivisions to the acreage on the city's edges.

Older houses in the historic districts often still have standard spigots without a vacuum breaker, which is an easy upgrade during any repair.

Backflow protection on outdoor faucets

An outdoor faucet with a hose attached is a potential cross-connection. If water pressure drops in the street, water in the hose, which may have been sitting in fertilizer or a pool, can be pulled back into the house lines.

A hose bib vacuum breaker stops that. It is a small device that screws onto the spigot, or comes built into most frost-free sillcocks. The City of Denton's cross-connection program treats backflow protection as a condition of water service, and outdoor faucets are one of the most common places it is missing.

If an older spigot has no vacuum breaker, adding one is a small, cheap upgrade. It is worth doing at the same time as any hose bib repair.

What outdoor faucet repair costs depend on

Most hose bib jobs are small.

  • Washer or cartridge repair versus a full valve replacement.
  • Upgrading to a frost-free model of the right length.
  • Access through brick, siding or an interior wall.
  • Any water damage inside the wall from a freeze split.

Pairing a hose bib repair with other small jobs on the same visit usually saves on the trip.
