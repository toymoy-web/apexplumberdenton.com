Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website or call the number on it, and how that information is used.

Call (940) 229-7314 All plumbing services

Information we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or payment pages, so it does not collect your name, email or address through the site itself.

When you call a phone number on this site, the call is handled through a call-tracking service. That service may record the calling number, the time and length of the call, the page or source that led to the call and, where permitted by law, a recording of the call. This information is used to route your call, measure which pages are useful and maintain service quality.

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message.

How information is shared

To handle your request, call details may be shared with the independent plumbing professional or service partner who responds to your call. We do not sell your personal information.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users or others.

Cookies and third-party content

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and displays maps embedded from Google Maps. When your browser loads those resources, Google may receive your IP address and set cookies according to its own privacy policy. The site does not use its own advertising cookies.

Your hosting provider and content delivery network may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and performance.

Your choices and contact

You can block cookies in your browser settings. You can ask about, correct or request deletion of call information associated with your phone number by emailing contact@apexplumberdenton.com.

This site is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
