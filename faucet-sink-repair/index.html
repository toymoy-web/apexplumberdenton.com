What a faucet and sink visit usually fixes

Faucet and sink repair covers everything from the spout to the trap. These are the jobs that come up most in Denton kitchens and baths.

  • Drips from the spout. A worn cartridge, washer or ceramic disc. Most brands still sell the exact replacement part.
  • Weak or uneven flow. Scale clogging the aerator or the cartridge itself, which is common on lake-sourced water.
  • Water under the sink. A failing braided supply hose, a loose compression nut, a cracked P-trap or a leaking disposal flange.
  • Handles that stick or spin. Stripped stems or broken handle adapters.
  • Pull-down sprayers that leak. Worn hose O-rings or a cracked quick-connect fitting.
  • Shutoff valves that will not close. Old multi-turn valves seize over time. Replacing them with quarter-turn valves is a small job that pays off in the next emergency.

A slow-draining basin is a clog, not a faucet problem. That job is covered on the drain cleaning and unclogging page.

Emergency faucet and sink repair for burst supply lines

A supply line that bursts under a sink can put a lot of water into a cabinet and floor in minutes. A cracked shutoff valve or a faucet that will not turn off is the same kind of emergency.

Close the two angle stops under the sink. If a stop is the part that failed or will not turn, close the main shutoff for the house and call the 24/7 line.

The emergency visit replaces the failed line or valve and checks the other connections under that sink, so one weak part does not become the next call.

Why Denton faucets wear the way they do

City of Denton water comes from Lewisville Lake and Ray Roberts Lake. Published hardness figures for Denton vary a lot, but white crust on aerators and showerheads is a common sight here. That same mineral film forms inside cartridges, where it scratches the seals and makes handles stiff.

Older homes near the Courthouse-on-the-Square and in the Oak-Hickory Historic District add another layer. Their supply stubs may be galvanized steel, and rust flakes from those pipes collect in aerators and cartridge screens.

Near UNT and TWU, rental kitchens get heavy daily use. A kitchen faucet in a four-bedroom student rental cycles far more often than one in a two-person household, so cartridges and sprayer hoses wear out sooner.

The practical upshot: before replacing a whole faucet, have the cartridge and aerator checked. On a quality faucet, a cartridge swap often restores it for a fraction of the cost.

Faucet repair: what happens during the visit

The plumber starts below the sink. Supply hoses, shutoff valves, the trap and the drain connection are checked for moisture with a light and a moisture meter, since a leak under the cabinet often starts somewhere other than where the puddle forms.

Next comes the faucet itself. The water is shut off, the handle is removed and the cartridge or stem is pulled. The brand and model are identified so the correct part goes back in. Scale is cleaned from the valve body before reassembly.

Any new faucet or supply part must be lead-free under the federal Reduction of Lead in Drinking Water Act, which since 2014 caps lead at 0.25 percent of wetted surfaces. Inside Denton city limits, the connection work itself follows the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) the City adopted in 2022.

Finally, the aerator gets cleaned or replaced, the supply lines are tested under full pressure and the cabinet is left dry. If a shutoff valve is seized, the plumber will say so and offer to replace it while the water is already off.

Water heater trouble can look like a faucet issue. If hot water comes out weak at every tap, not just one, the cause is more likely at the tank, which is covered under water heater repair.

If water is spreading under the sink right now

These steps work in almost every kitchen and bathroom.

  • Clear the cabinet so you can see the two small valves on the wall or floor.
  • Turn both valves clockwise until they stop. If they will not turn, close the house main.
  • Put a shallow pan or towel under the drip and leave the cabinet doors open to dry.
  • Take a photo of the leaking part and any model label on the faucet. It helps the plumber bring the right part.

Fixture and plumbing installation

Installation calls cover new faucets, sinks, supply lines, shutoff valves, ice maker lines and laundry connections. A new fixture is only as good as the valves and lines behind it, so worn angle stops and old braided lines are usually replaced at the same visit.

If you have bought the fixture yourself, have the box on hand when you call. The plumber can confirm the connections fit your existing plumbing before the visit.

What changes the price of faucet and sink repair

Most of these jobs are small, but a few details move the price.

  • Part availability. A common cartridge costs little. A discontinued or designer model can take special ordering.
  • Seized valves or corroded fittings. These add time and parts, especially in older homes.
  • Countertop access. Undermount sinks and tight vanities take longer to work under.
  • Fixture supplied by you or by the plumber. Either works, but a fixture you supply should be confirmed compatible first.

If a faucet leak has damaged the cabinet or subfloor, homeowner insurance may cover that damage if it happened suddenly. Slow leaks usually are not covered.
