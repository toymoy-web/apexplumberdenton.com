Sewer Line Repair in Denton, TX

A sewer line that backs up every few months, a sewage smell in the yard or a soft spot in the lawn after dry weather. Sewer line repair in Denton, TX fixes the damaged section of your lateral, after a camera shows exactly what is wrong, and backups that are flooding a house get a 24/7 response.

Signs the sewer line itself is damaged

A clog can be cleared. A damaged line keeps clogging. These signs point to the pipe, not just what is inside it.

  • Backups that return within weeks of a cleaning.
  • A sewage odor outdoors, often near the cleanout or along the line's path.
  • A lush or soggy strip of lawn over the line when the rest of the yard is dry.
  • Gurgling toilets and slow drains throughout the house.
  • Cracks or settling in the slab or driveway near where the line runs.

What damages sewer laterals in Denton

Sewer line repair is the work of fixing a damaged section of the private line that carries waste from the house to the city main, without replacing the whole run. In Denton, three things cause most of that damage.

The first is ground movement. Expansive clay under much of the city swells and shrinks with the weather. A lateral moving with the soil can crack, pull apart at a joint or sag into a belly where waste settles.

The second is roots. Post oaks, pecans and cedar elms are common in older Denton neighborhoods, and their roots find any open joint. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches everything.

The third is age. Homes built decades ago may still have clay tile or cast iron laterals. Clay joints leak, and cast iron corrodes from the inside until the bottom of the pipe wears through.

Finding the problem before digging

New pipe is laid to the minimum slopes in the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), such as 1/8 inch of fall per foot for the 4-inch pipe common in home sewer laterals. Every repair should start with a sewer camera inspection. The plumber feeds a camera through the cleanout, records the line and marks the distance to each defect. A locator on the surface then shows exactly where to dig.

If the line is full of roots or grease, it may need cleaning first so the camera can see. Hydro jetting often serves both purposes: it clears the line and gives the camera a clean look at the pipe wall.

In Denton, the homeowner usually owns the lateral from the house to the city main, and the city maintains the main. If the camera shows the problem is at or past the main connection, the City of Denton wastewater department should be the next call.

What sewer line repair costs depend on

These factors swing the price the most.

  • Depth and location of the damage, especially under a driveway, patio or slab.
  • Repair method. Spot repairs cost less than lining or bursting a long section.
  • Pipe material and size.
  • Restoration of landscaping, concrete or flooring after the repair.
  • Permits and inspections required by the City of Denton.

Most homeowner policies do not cover sewer line wear or root damage, and many exclude sewer backups unless an endorsement was added. Check before assuming.
