Plumbing Service Area: Denton, TX and Nearby Towns

Our plumbers cover all of Denton and seven nearby towns in Denton County. This page lists the areas, the ZIP codes and what tends to be different about the plumbing in each place.

Inside the city of Denton

Every Denton ZIP code is inside the 24/7 service area: 76201, 76205, 76207, 76208, 76209 and 76210. That runs from the blocks around the Courthouse-on-the-Square and the Oak-Hickory Historic District to the neighborhoods near UNT and TWU, south to Robson Ranch and east past Loop 288.

Older homes near downtown are the most likely to have galvanized supply pipe and cast iron drains. Rentals around the two universities see heavy use of disposals, toilets and kitchen drains. Newer subdivisions on the edges of town run into slab leaks and water heater wear as the clay beneath them settles.

Inside Denton city limits, plumbing work follows the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), which the City adopted in 2022 with regional amendments. Nearby towns adopt their own editions and amendments, so permit rules vary slightly from town to town. The plumber pulls the permit from whichever city has jurisdiction.

Denton's own water comes from Lewisville Lake and Ray Roberts Lake through the City of Denton utility. The surrounding towns get their water from a patchwork of providers, which is one reason plumbing problems differ from one town to the next.

What to tell the dispatcher when you call

These details help send the right plumber with the right equipment.

  • Your ZIP code and nearest cross street. Rural addresses can be hard to find at night.
  • City water or a private well, and city sewer or septic.
  • What you see: where the water is, which fixtures are affected, and when it started.
  • Whether the water is shut off, and whether anyone needs access help, such as a gate code.
  • If you rent, the landlord or property manager's contact.

If water is actively escaping, close the main shutoff first. Our guide to a burst pipe emergency shows where to find it.
